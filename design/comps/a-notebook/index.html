About this notebook

Plotline is Luyen Chou's personal site of plots: small, self-contained interactive data visualizers about current events, each built from primary sources. It is a way of understanding the world through data, one entry at a time, and each plot opens with a short note on why it was made.

There are no accounts and no tracking. The pages are static, hosted on GitHub Pages, and every figure cites where it came from.

Each plot shows an "Updated" date, and the numbers on it refresh with their sources.
