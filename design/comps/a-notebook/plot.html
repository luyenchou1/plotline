Debt is the total public debt outstanding, Treasury's daily count. GDP is nominal, quarterly, from FRED.

Both figures are in trillions of nominal dollars, which is why they can share one axis.

The crossing is the reason this plot exists. From Feb 2013 the debt line sits above GDP, and the shaded gap is the ratio: 123% today.

The live plot zooms across all thirty-three years; the milestones along the line are the laws and shocks that bent the curve.

Every point on the line is a day in Treasury's count.
