Reading this page

The counts are built from two public files kept by the nflverse project (CC BY 4.0): the league's weekly injury reports since 2009 and the weekly rosters. A player-week is lost when a player is listed Out on the report for that week's game, or when he is on a reserve list that week (injured reserve, physically unable to perform, non-football injury); a player on both is counted once. Listings that are not injuries, such as illness or personal matters, are left out, and so is the COVID reserve list where the roster data marks it (2021 on; 2020 cannot be separated). Only the regular season is counted.

Two things to hold in mind. The weekly report only names players on the active roster, and its rules changed in 2016, when "Probable" was dropped and reporting was tightened. The reserve-list series is thin before 2016 in the roster data, so those years are shown faded and the long-run comparison should be read from the Out series. Rule changes that move injuries between the two lists are marked in gold.

The league's own injury numbers, compiled by IQVIA and published each February, cover concussions, ACL tears, lower-body strains and games missed from 2015; they are here, and they are not machine-readable, so they are cited in the notes rather than plotted. This page refreshes from the nflverse files on a schedule; in season that means every week.
